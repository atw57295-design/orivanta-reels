# ORIVANTA — Official Brand Identity (v3, approved)

**Status: LOCKED.** This identity was approved by the brand owner on 2026-11-12, based on
two user-supplied ChatGPT-generated references:
- `orivanta_logo_circle_approved.png` (1254×1254) — the official logo mark.
- `orivanta_banner_reference.png` (2172×724) — a mood-board / style reference only.
  **Not a final deliverable at these dimensions** — it does not match any real platform's
  banner spec and is a multi-image collage. It is used here only to confirm palette,
  typography and visual-style continuity with the logo.

**Rule going forward: the logo mark itself is not to be redesigned, recolored, or
re-proportioned without the user's explicit permission.** Every platform asset below
is a new, correctly-dimensioned composition built *around* this locked mark and palette
— never a resize/crop/stretch of the collage reference, and never a modification of the
logo's own geometry.

---

## 1. Logo mark

- File: `orivanta_logo_circle_approved.png`, 1254×1254 px, circular badge on a transparent-safe
  square canvas.
- Structure (top → bottom, as a % of the circle's diameter):
  1. 0–38%: a globe wrapped in a film-strip motif carrying small cinematic vignettes
     (pyramids, Roman columns, classical ruins) — the "world of stories" icon.
  2. 38–58%: the **ORIVANTA** wordmark, engraved metallic gold, the dominant element.
  3. 58–62%: a small gold compass/star divider glyph.
  4. 62–70%: small-caps subtitle, "AI-POWERED STORIES & ENTERTAINMENT".
  5. 70–75%: italic tagline, "Stories Beyond Ordinary".
  6. 75–100%: a cinematic base vignette — generic ancient ruins (left), a domed historic
     city skyline (center), a sailing ship (right). Generic/architectural only — no named
     or recognizable historical figures, consistent with the "no specific historical
     characters in the permanent identity" rule.
- Border: a double concentric gold ring frames the full circle.
- **Clear space:** keep empty margin ≥ 10% of the logo's diameter on all sides in any
  composition.
- **Minimum size:** do not display the full badge (with legible wordmark + subtitle) below
  ~120 px diameter. Below that, use a simplified mark (globe + compass only, no wordmark/
  subtitle) for favicons / app icons — to be produced as a separate "mark-only" asset,
  not yet built.

## 2. Color palette (extracted by pixel/cluster sampling from both references)

| Role | Swatch | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy (primary background) | navy | `#0B1B28` | Dominant background across both refs (sampled range `#03141F`–`#0A151D`). |
| Void Black (deepest shadow / vignette falloff) | black | `#030509` | Corner/edge darkening, frame backgrounds. |
| Antique Gold (primary metallic accent — ring, wordmark base) | gold | `#C9A24B` | Core metallic gold (sampled range `#C29764`–`#DD9043`). |
| Bright Gold Highlight (specular edge on engraved metal) | light gold | `#E8B86D` | Highlight side of the beveled wordmark and ring. |
| Bronze / Copper (shadow side of the metallic gold) | bronze | `#7A4A24` | Darker metal shadow tone (sampled range `#6C4D30`–`#905E2F`). |
| Ivory (supporting text, subtitle) | ivory | `#F2EFE6` | Warm off-white for small-caps subtitle text; the reference uses a near-white (~`#FAFAFA`) for crisp legibility at small sizes — use `#F2EFE6` for anything larger/warmer (cards, lower-thirds) and near-white only for tiny caption-scale text. |

**Usage rule:** Navy/Black are always the base; Gold/Bronze are the only accent family;
Ivory is reserved for body/support text. No other hues (no blue-accent, no red, no teal)
belong in the permanent identity — this keeps every asset visually continuous with the
reference-channel study (Fall of Civilizations / Real Engineering / Kings and Generals /
Wendover Productions / Curious Droid all use a similarly restrained 3–4-tone palette).

## 3. Typography

| Role | Style observed | Practical production font | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark "ORIVANTA" | Engraved/beveled metallic capitals, classical Roman inscriptional serif, wide tracking, ornamental serif flourish on R/A | **Cinzel** (Bold/Black weight) | Closest freely-licensed match to the squared, inscriptional serif in the reference; apply a gold gradient + bevel/emboss effect to reproduce the engraved metal look. |
| Subtitle ("AI-POWERED STORIES & ENTERTAINMENT") | Clean small-caps serif, wide letter-spacing, ivory/near-white | **Cinzel** (Regular) | Same type family as the wordmark for consistency, lighter weight, no metallic effect — flat ivory fill. |
| Tagline ("Stories Beyond Ordinary") | Italic serif, lighter weight, gold | **EB Garamond Italic** or **Playfair Display Italic** | Softer, more classical italic than Cinzel (which has no true italic) — used only for this one tagline line. |
| Body/caption text (film subtitles, lower-thirds) | (unchanged, separate from brand mark) | **Liberation Sans** | Already in use for burned-in subtitles — a plain sans stays correct for on-screen readability; it is not part of the decorative brand type system. |

## 4. What this locks in vs. what's still pending

**Locked now (do not change without explicit approval):**
- The logo mark geometry, composition, and the five brand colors above.
- The two-typeface system (Cinzel for the wordmark/subtitle family, a serif italic for the
  tagline).
- The rule that no named historical figure appears in permanent branding — only generic
  architecture, landscapes, and objects.

None of these exist as final assets yet. The attached banner reference is explicitly a
mood board, not used as-is for any platform.

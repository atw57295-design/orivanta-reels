But remember what else nitrate made.

Germany had no nitrate deposits of its own. Most of what it used — for its fields, and for its explosives — came by sea, from Chile.

In the summer of 1914, Europe went to war. And the sea lanes were about to close.

Robert Le Rossignol, the British engineer who had built the first machine, was still living in Germany. When war broke out, he was interned as an enemy alien — and later released to work for a German company for the rest of the war.
